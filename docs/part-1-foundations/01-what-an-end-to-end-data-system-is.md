# 1. What an end-to-end data system is

**In one line:** it takes data from where it is created and turns it into something people and software can trust.

**Think of it like:** a water network. Water is collected, cleaned, stored and piped to homes.

Orchestration, governance and observability apply at every stage, like the staff and inspectors who keep a water network running.

**Remember:** data flows one way, and it becomes more trustworthy at each stage.
