# 3. Requirements

**In one line:** requirements say what the system must do and how well it must do it.

**Think of it like:** planning a house. You ask how many people will live there and what the budget is before drawing anything.

**Capacity estimation** is a rough size calculation: events per day multiplied by event size. For example, 10 million events at 1 KB each is about 10 GB a day.

**Remember:** real-time data costs more, so ask who will act on it and how quickly.
