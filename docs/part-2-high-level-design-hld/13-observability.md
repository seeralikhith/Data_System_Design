# 13. Observability

**In one line:** observability tells you whether data is on time, complete and correct, ideally before users notice a problem.

**Think of it like:** a car dashboard. The dials show what is happening, and warning lights tell you when to act.

**Remember:** alert only when someone must act. Too many alerts cause **alert fatigue**, and people start ignoring the real ones.
