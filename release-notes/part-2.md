## Part 2: High-level design (HLD)

Part 2 covers the big choices in a data system: what it must do, how data moves through it, where it lives, who uses it, and how it stays reliable, secure and affordable.

**Read it online:** https://seeralikhith.github.io/Data_System_Design/
